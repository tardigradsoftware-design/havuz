# E-Commerce Conversion & Baymard UX Benchmarks

## 1. Critical Industry Benchmarks

- **Average Cart Abandonment Rate**: ~70.19% across industries.
- **Top Reason for Abandonment**: Extra costs (shipping, tax, fees) too high (48%).
- **Second Reason**: Account creation required before checkout (26%).
- **Third Reason**: Slow or confusing checkout process (21%).

---

## 2. High-Impact PDP Optimizations

1. **Sticky Mobile Add-to-Cart Bar**: Increases mobile add-to-cart conversions by up to 12%.
2. **Estimated Delivery Date**: Providing an exact date ("Arrives Thursday, Sep 18") converts 8% higher than vague ranges ("Ships in 3-5 days").
3. **Dynamic Free Shipping Threshold**: "Add $12 to qualify for Free Shipping" increases Average Order Value (AOV) by 15–20%.

---

## 3. Checkout Best Practices

- Strip header and footer navigation from checkout pages.
- Auto-complete shipping addresses via address lookup APIs.
- Provide 1-click Express Checkout (Apple Pay, Google Pay).
- Retain cart items for at least 30 days.
